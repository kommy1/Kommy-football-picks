✈ KOMMY Aviator Learning Centre

Understand multipliers, randomness and probability — without fake signals or betting recommendations.

Important: This page does not generate Aviator signals, predict the next round, or recommend bets. Past results cannot reliably tell you what will happen next.

How a multiplier works

A multiplier is a number shown during a round. For example, 2.00× means twice the starting amount in the game's displayed multiplier. It is a description of that round, not a promise about a future round.

Each round should be treated as a new uncertain event. A previous low or high multiplier does not mean the next one is “due” to be high or low.

Probability basics

Randomness

Random outcomes can form streaks. A streak does not prove that a pattern will continue or reverse.

Independent rounds

If rounds are generated independently, earlier results do not determine the next result.

No guaranteed signal

A chart, colour, sequence or “AI signal” cannot guarantee the next multiplier.

Beware of claims

Be careful with anyone selling guaranteed signals, VIP access or certain-win predictions.

Common myths

Myth: “After several low results, a high result must come.”

Fact: A run of previous results does not make a particular next result due.

Myth: “A signal provider can know the next crash point.”

Fact: Without access to a verified mechanism that reveals future outcomes, such claims are not reliable.

Myth: “A past-results table predicts the next round.”

Fact: A history table only describes what has already happened.
